How this works (30 seconds)
  1. Paste your list straight from Excel — three columns: description, cost, supplier stock code (code is optional). Up to 50 lines at a time.
  2. Check my list tidies the names and looks for items that already exist, so you don't create duplicates.
  3. Review each line — use an existing code, or confirm it's new and pick its sub-category.
  4. Send — download the CSV and/or open the email. Nothing is sent until you press Send in Outlook.
